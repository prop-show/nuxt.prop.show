## Overall Impression

The publication voice and the restraint hold up: content carries the interface, and the yellow panel is the one place the design raises its hand. The two real risks are continuity and guidance — the site forgets where you came from, and the 24-part course assumes you arrived for episode 0. A heading-order regression introduced in this session's layout work is the one thing that must be fixed before shipping.

## What's Working

- Real publication hierarchy: large titles, quiet counts, generous rows; almost no decoration competes with comprehension.
- Destination handling: platform buttons and repository actions sit near the title and name where they go.
- Coherent responsive foundation: mobile rails hold, long titles wrap naturally, the expanded tag bank has no horizontal overflow.

## Priority Issues

- **[P1] Archive pages skip a heading level.** `/videos` (and, by the same structure, `/news` and `/works`) now go `h1` → `h3`: the list section lost its `h2` when the duplicate “全部视频/全部速报/全部作品” heading was removed. Why it matters: screen-reader heading navigation jumps a level and the list loses its named region. Fix: keep a visually hidden `h2` (`sr-only`) naming the list, leaving the visible count caption unchanged. Suggested: `$impeccable audit`.
- **[P2] The archive return drops the reader's place.** `/videos?category=编程技巧` → episode → “返回视频” lands on `/videos` with 全部分类 selected. Fix: carry the originating URL (page + filters) into the return link, with a plain archive fallback for direct visits. Suggested: `$impeccable harden`.
- **[P2] The course directory treats every visit as episode 0.** 24 undifferentiated rows, ~4042px tall on mobile, every row repeating the same metadata. Fix: group the existing sequence into project phases with compact anchors, keep “从前言开始学习” as the novice path. Suggested: `$impeccable layout`.
- **[P2] Mobile home promotes the course before the updates.** The stacked course panel pushes “视频” below ~862px and “速报” below ~1246px at 390px width, weakening the catch-up use case. Fix: compress the mobile course entry to a title + link under the featured program. Suggested: `$impeccable layout`.
- **[P2] Source visibility is inconsistent where credibility is judged.** The Prisma brief makes quantitative claims but its original link appears only at the article's end, and only one news document supplies `letter.source`. Fix: surface the existing original link beside the date metadata. Suggested: `$impeccable layout`.
- **[P2] Recovery and pagination targets are smaller than the site's own comfort floor.** “清除筛选” measures 56×20 (verified independently); pagination controls 36px. Fix: ≥44px hit areas without enlarging type. Suggested: `$impeccable adapt`.

## Persona Red Flags

**Alex (returning reader/learner)**: “返回视频” resets the chosen category; the course offers only “从前言开始学习” or the full directory, so reaching the JWT/Redis stage needs manual scanning every time.

**Sam (keyboard/accessibility)**: no skip-to-main link; the only live region reports loading (`layout.vue:26`), so a filter change gives no announcement; heading order breaks on all three archives.

**Casey (mobile, one-handed)**: 20px clear-filter and 36px pagination targets demand precision; the long secondary home promotion delays quick news scanning.
